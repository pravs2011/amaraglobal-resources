Our Story

Everlasting Commitment to Excellence

The name AMARA means “EVERLASTING,” reflecting our commitment to growth and long-term value for partners, clients, and employees alike.

Our commitment to excellence and sustainability drives us to create value for all those who are associated with us with an aim to position us as a trusted business partner.

By fostering a culture of excellence, inclusion and continuous improvement, we ensure that our processes, services, and outcomes remain efficient, reliable, and competitive. We constantly strive to provide effective solutions by leveraging our business knowledge, best in class practices and technical expertise as we believe in continuous research, learning and exploration to provide innovative products.

As an upcoming young leader in refining and trading essential minerals in Zambia, Tanzania, Guinea Conakry, we aim to deliver quality minerals with sustainable practices, Integrated refining and trading across multiple commodities.
