Our Story

Everlasting

The name "Amara" means "Everlasting" — a reflection of our enduring commitment to ethical practices, environmental responsibility, and sustainable growth across the African continent.

Founded in 2024 and headquartered in Lusaka, Zambia, Amara Global Resources LTD is a dynamic and diversified player in the African energy, mining, engineering, and infrastructure sectors. We have rapidly built a reputation for reliability, integrity, and operational excellence.
