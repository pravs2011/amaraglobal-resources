Our Story

Everlasting
Commitment to
Excellence

The name AMARA means “EVERLASTING,” reflecting our commitment to growth and long-term value for partners, clients, and employees alike.

We are a young, dynamic and forward-thinking organization committed to delivering high-quality, sustainable building products that meet the evolving needs of our clients. As a business, we look forward to have an everlasting association with our partners, clients and employees by seamlessly integrating global expertise with local insights. Our commitment to excellence and sustainability drives us to create value for all those who are associated with us with an aim to position us as a trusted business partner.

We are fortunate to position ourselves in some the world's fastest-growing economies, drawn by increasingly diverse markets and infrastructure development under the patronage of their visionary leaders who have put the country on the growth map in a brief span of time.

Built on a strong foundation of integrity, innovation, and excellence, we position ourselves to grow into a trusted and reliable partner. Our journey has been driven by a clear vision of our partners who have created sustainable value for our customers while continuously adapting to market trends and technological advancements. The management team consists of professionals that have worked in “C” levels with various large corporation globally. Our experienced team of experts, leverage cutting-edge technology and data-driven strategies to deliver reliable, efficient, and sustainable outcomes.

We aim to maintain the highest standards of professionalism and accountability in every aspect of our operations. By fostering a culture of excellence, inclusion and continuous improvement, we ensure that our processes, services, and outcomes remain efficient, reliable, and competitive. We constantly strive to provide effective solutions by leveraging our business knowledge, best in class practices and technical expertise as we believe in continuous research, learning and exploration to provide innovative products.

As an upcoming young leader in refining and trading essential minerals in Zambia, Tanzania, Guinea Conakry, we aim to deliver quality minerals with sustainable practices, Integrated refining and trading across multiple commodities.
